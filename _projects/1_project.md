<div>
  <h3>Overview</h3>
  <p>HoverNotes is a U.S. company and privacy-first AI video note-taking product that I operate remotely from Seoul. It turns online lectures, tutorials, and course videos into structured notes users can keep in their own Obsidian/Markdown workflows. I founded and built the product across browser-extension workflows, a Next.js web app, AI note-generation systems, payments, analytics, localization, SEO, and production operations.</p>

  <h3>Current Scale</h3>
  <ul>
    <li>Over <strong>$10K/month revenue</strong> as of June 2026.</li>
    <li>Around <strong>20K installs</strong> across Chrome and Edge.</li>
    <li>Global monetization through Stripe, WeChat Pay/Alipay one-time passes, and Razorpay/UPI.</li>
  </ul>

  <h3>What I Built</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Video-in, notes-out AI workflow:</strong> the extension captures realtime video learning context, the web app deduplicates and ranks visual frames, and the backend generates structured notes from transcript plus selected visual context.</li>
    <li><strong>Local-first note-taking:</strong> Obsidian vault saving, screenshot handling, note history, reconnect/recovery paths, and guardrails against destructive auto-save behavior.</li>
    <li><strong>Regional payments:</strong> Stripe for global card payments, WeChat Pay/Alipay one-time passes for China, and Razorpay/UPI for India.</li>
    <li><strong>Product analytics:</strong> activation, paywall behavior, platform-specific drop-off, media payload size, warning telemetry, and AI Notes session health.</li>
    <li><strong>Growth and reliability systems:</strong> SEO/content infrastructure, localization, regional pricing, billing reliability, release management, and production observability.</li>
  </ul>

  <h3>Stack</h3>
  <p>Next.js, React, TypeScript, browser extensions, Lexical, Python/FastAPI, PostgreSQL/Drizzle, Stripe, Razorpay, PostHog, Vercel, Cloudflare R2, and AI/LLM workflows.</p>
